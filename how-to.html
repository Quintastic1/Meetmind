Uploading a Call Three ways to get your recording into Callforge

METHOD 1 📁 Direct File Upload
→ Go to Upload tab in your dashboard
→ Tap the upload zone or Browse files
→ Select your audio or video file
→ Callforge processes it automatically
Supports: MP3, MP4, M4A, WAV, WebM, OGG, AAC, FLAC
METHOD 2 📧 Email Upload
→ Go to Upload tab → save "Callforge Upload" contact
→ Open Voice Memos after your call
→ Share → Mail → To: Callforge Upload
→ Hit send — results appear in ~2 min
Best for iPhone users — works from any app
METHOD 3 📱 Install as App (PWA)
→ Open Safari → callforge.to/dashboard.html
→ Tap Share → Add to Home Screen
→ Callforge icon appears on home screen
→ Upload directly from phone camera roll
Stays logged in — fastest mobile experience
📱 iPhone tip: If your recording is stored in iCloud and appears greyed out — long press the file → Download — wait for it to fully download to your phone, then upload. iCloud files must be local before uploading.

Understanding Your Results What Callforge generates from every call

🎯

Deal Health Score (0-100)

An overall score for how likely this deal is to close based on the conversation. 80-100 = strong buying signals. 60-79 = interested but friction. 40-59 = lukewarm. Under 40 = significant obstacles.

📝

AI Summary

A 3-4 sentence summary of what happened on the call — key points discussed, the current status of the deal, and what the prospect cares about most. Generated in the language of the call.

✅

Action Items

Every commitment made on the call is extracted and listed. Each action item includes the owner and deadline if mentioned. Tap any checkbox to mark it complete.

📧

AI-Generated Follow-up Email

A personalized follow-up email drafted in the voice of the call — warm, specific, and ready to send. Tap "Copy email" to copy it to your clipboard. Available in English and Spanish.

✏️ Always personalize before sending — the AI gives you a strong draft, you add the human touch
⏱ Processing takes 45 seconds to 2 minutes depending on the length of your call. You'll receive an email notification at your Callforge address when results are ready.

HubSpot Integration Sync every call to your CRM automatically

1

Get your HubSpot Private App token

In HubSpot → Settings → Integrations → Private Apps → Create a private app. Name it "Callforge". Add scopes: contacts read/write, deals read/write. Copy the token (starts with pat-na2-).

2

Connect in Callforge Settings

Go to Settings tab in your dashboard → scroll to HubSpot CRM Integration → paste your token → click Connect HubSpot. You'll see a green ✅ Connected badge.

3

Sync any meeting to HubSpot

Open any processed meeting → scroll to the bottom → tap "🔗 Sync to HubSpot". Callforge will find or create the contact, update the deal stage, log the call summary as a note, and create a task for each action item.

🔄 Your token is saved — connect once, works forever across all devices
What gets synced: Contact (found or created by name/email) · Deal (created with score-based pipeline stage) · Call summary as engagement note · One task per action item

Using on iPhone The easiest ways to upload from your phone

RECOMMENDED 📧 Email Method
1 Dashboard → Upload tab → tap "Save as Phone Contact"
2 "Callforge Upload ⚒" is now in your contacts
3 After any call → Voice Memos → Share → Mail
4 To: "Callforge Upload" → Send
5 Results appear in dashboard in ~2 minutes
ALTERNATIVE 📱 PWA Upload
1 Install Callforge: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
2 Open Callforge from your home screen
3 Tap Upload tab
4 Browse files → select your recording
5 Tap to upload — stays logged in automatically
⚠️ iCloud files: If a recording shows as greyed out in the file picker, it's still in iCloud. Long press → Download, wait for it to download to your phone, then upload. Files must be stored locally to upload.
📱 Recording tip: Use iPhone's built-in Voice Memos app to record calls on speaker. Ask the prospect's permission first — "Would you mind if I recorded this so I can remember our conversation?" Most people say yes.

Frequently Asked Questions The stuff everyone wants to know

What languages does Callforge support?
English and Spanish, fully. Callforge automatically detects which language was spoken on the call and delivers your summary, action items, deal score, follow-up email, and Forged coaching all in that language. No setup required.
What file formats are supported?
MP3, MP4, M4A, WAV, WebM, OGG, AAC, FLAC, CAF, and MOV. iPhone Voice Memos records in M4A — that works perfectly.
How long does processing take?
Typically 45 seconds to 2 minutes depending on the length of your call. A 30-minute call usually processes in about 90 seconds. You'll get an email when it's ready.
Is my call data private?
Yes. Your recordings and transcripts are stored securely in your private account. We don't share your data with other users or third parties. You can delete any meeting at any time.
Do I need to tell the other person I'm recording?
Yes — and we strongly recommend it. Recording laws vary by state and country. A simple "Would you mind if I recorded this so I can remember our conversation?" is professional, builds trust, and is legally safe. Most people say yes.
Does it work with Zoom and Google Meet?
Yes — download your Zoom or Google Meet recording after the call and upload it to Callforge. Native Zoom bot integration (automatic recording without downloading) is coming soon.
Can I use Callforge for my whole team?
Absolutely. The Team plan supports up to 10 seats at $299/month with $25 per seat after that. Each team member gets their own dashboard, meetings, and Forged coaching.
What if the AI gets something wrong?
The AI is very accurate but not perfect — treat the output as a strong first draft, not gospel. Always review action items and the follow-up email before acting on them. If you find consistent errors, reach out to us at hello@callforge.to.
How do I cancel?
Email hello@callforge.to and we'll cancel immediately. No lock-ins, no questions asked. Your data remains accessible for 30 days after cancellation.
